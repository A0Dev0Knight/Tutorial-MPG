## Describing my journey:

I was stuck in tutorial hell; I couldn't think for myself about how to develop something and was always constrained by what others had made online.

It all changed when I was tasked with creating a 2048 game in C. After this, I got the courage to try Unity again.

This time, I was determined to reach an end goal: to have a game made from A to Z by myself. I started by getting to know the language better and what it had to offer. So I did, following the first two tutorials mentioned above. While waiting for the advanced C# course from this guy, I decided to put my newly acquired skills to good use.

It took me roughly 3 weeks to finish the game. I took it slow and learned how to do things properly.

The hardest part of this journey was the end. I had been following the course for so long that I lost motivation and felt like starting a new game. If you struggle, I recommend starting a second project before finishing your first one to help you get through the rough patches. In my case, I felt the desire to work on a car controller. Had I done it, I might have finished the course faster, but faster is not always better. Remember that.

In other words, it was a good learning resource. I haven't learned everything to the letter, but I will come back to it when I get stuck—that is to be expected, after all.

We shall meet again and see if I manage to make a name for myself after all.


## What i have added:

1. `Trash counter` has a `fill amount` - press `F` key to empty it.

2. `Respawn` Function: Sometimes, the player may phase through objects. Press the `R` key to respawn at position `(0, 0, 0)`.

3. `Retry` / `Main` menu `buttons` when game is over

4. `Number` of `successful` / `failed recipes` on game over screen.
